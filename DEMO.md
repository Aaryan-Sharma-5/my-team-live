# Two-minute demo outline

1. **Fan problem (20 seconds):** During a match, the broadcast follows the game, but a fantasy fan cares about the 11 people in their own team. It is difficult to know which events changed their total and why.
2. **Show the replay (40 seconds):** Open the app, point out the replay and simplified-scoring labels, select a captain or swap a player, and start. Speed up the match and show a boundary or wicket in the personalized feed.
3. **Explain trust (25 seconds):** Expand a player and the scoring rules. Walk through a captain's event: code computes each contribution and applies the multiplier. The language model only phrases structured facts; it doesn't calculate the score.
4. **Show resilience (15 seconds):** Explain that if the optional model is unavailable, template narration appears and the replay continues.
5. **Trade-offs and next steps (20 seconds):** One recorded match, no rank or live data, and transparent simplified rules kept the scope testable. With authorized production data, connect the official points engine and live feed, then explore rank changes and timely notifications.
